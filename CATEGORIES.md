# Categories for Custom Lumos

This file defines all categories used in this repository.

---

## 💼 Productivity

Lumos for work, automation, business, and planning.

**Examples:** Coding Assistant, Meeting Summarizer, Project Planner, Data Formatter

**Tags:** `productivity`, `business`, `automation`, `work`

---

## 🛠️ Tech Support

Buying guides, hardware/software advice, debugging, and setup help.

**Examples:** Hardware Advisor, Software Comparison, Debug Helper, Setup Guide

**Tags:** `tech`, `hardware`, `software`, `debugging`, `buying-guides`

---

## 😄 Funny

Humorous, entertaining, pranks.

**Examples:** Taylor Swift Fan, Comedian, Meme Generator

**Tags:** `funny`, `humor`, `entertainment`

---

## 💬 Empathetic

Emotional support, listening, coaching.

**Examples:** Empathy Buddy, Therapeutic Listener, Life Coach

**Tags:** `empathetic`, `support`, `wellness`

**⚠️ Note:** This does not replace professional therapy.

---

## 🎨 Creative

Art, creative writing, music, design.

**Examples:** Writing Coach, Art Critic, Music Analyst

**Tags:** `creative`, `art`, `writing`

---

## 🧠 Learning

Education, tutoring, knowledge acquisition.

**Examples:** Math Tutor, Language Partner, History Guide

**Tags:** `learning`, `education`, `tutorial`

---

## 🎭 Roleplay

Characters, fictional personas, roleplaying.

**Examples:** Harry Potter Character, Historical Figure, Anime Character

**Tags:** `roleplay`, `character`, `fiction`

**⚠️ Note:** Impersonating real people requires mandatory disclaimers.

---

## 🌈 Open

Everything that doesn't fit elsewhere or is mixed.

**Tags:** `misc`, `open`
